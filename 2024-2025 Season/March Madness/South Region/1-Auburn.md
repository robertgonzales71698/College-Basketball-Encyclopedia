<h1 align="center">Auburn Tigers</h1>

## General Information
- **Conference:** Southeastern Conference (SEC)
- **Record:** 28-5 overall, 15-3 in SEC play
- **Coach:** Bruce Pearl

## Playstyle & Tendencies
- **Offensive Style:** Auburn employs a fast-paced, high-scoring offense, averaging approximately 83.8 points per game. The team effectively utilizes ball movement to create open shots, leading to a high field goal percentage of 48.3%. They also excel in three-point shooting, with a 36.8% success rate from beyond the arc.
- **Defensive Scheme:** The Tigers' defense is characterized by aggressive perimeter play, limiting opponents to a 29.2% shooting percentage from three-point range. They also excel in disrupting opposing offenses, leading the nation in assists allowed per game (8.2).
- **Key Strengths:**
  - High-scoring offense with multiple players capable of leading in points.
  - Effective inside presence with dominant post players.
  - Strong three-point shooting capability.
  - Defensive prowess in limiting opponent assists and three-point shooting.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Occasional turnovers leading to scoring droughts.
  - Inconsistent shooting, particularly during critical moments.
  - Challenges in maintaining leads, requiring improved game management.
