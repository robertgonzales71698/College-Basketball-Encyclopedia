## General Information
- **Conference:** SEC  
- **Record:** 22-10 (11-7 SEC)  
- **Coach:** Buzz Williams  

## Playstyle & Tendencies
- **Offensive Style:** Balanced attack with emphasis on inside scoring and efficient ball movement.  
- **Defensive Scheme:** Aggressive man-to-man defense, focusing on creating turnovers and fast-break opportunities.  
- **Key Strengths:**  
  - High field goal percentage, leading the nation at 51.1%.
  - Strong rebounding, particularly on the offensive glass.  
  - Effective free-throw shooting.
- **Weaknesses:**  
  - Occasional turnovers due to high-pressure defense.  
  - Inconsistent outside shooting.

## Outlook
Texas A&M has demonstrated a well-rounded game this season, combining efficient offense with a stout defense. Their high field goal percentage and strong rebounding have been pivotal to their success. While their aggressive defense leads to turnovers, it also fuels their fast-break opportunities.
