## General Information
- **Conference:** Big 12  
- **Record:** 24-9 (13-7 Big 12)

## Playstyle & Tendencies
- **Offensive Style:** Disciplined half-court offense, heavy pick-and-roll usage  
- **Defensive Scheme:** Physical man-to-man with high-pressure trapping  
- **Key Strengths:**  
  - Elite defensive efficiency  
  - Forces a high number of turnovers  
  - Strong rebounding team  
- **Weaknesses:**  
  - Inconsistent outside shooting  
  - Struggles against teams that handle pressure well

## Outlook
Iowa State boasts one of the toughest defenses in the country, making them a nightmare matchup for opponents. Their ability to force turnovers and dictate the tempo will be key to their success. However, if their three-point shooting goes cold, they could struggle against teams that handle pressure well. A deep run is possible if their defense dominates, but offensive inconsistency remains a concern.
