## General Information

## Playstyle & Tendencies
- **Offensive Style:** Under Coach Dusty May, Michigan has embraced an up-tempo, high-scoring approach, averaging nearly 82 points per game. The offense emphasizes teamwork, efficient ball movement, and a balanced attack, featuring both strong inside play from their seven-footers and a proficient three-point shooting game.
- **Defensive Scheme:** The team employs a man-to-man defense, focusing on creating turnovers and limiting opponent scoring opportunities. While their defense has been effective at times, maintaining leads has been a challenge, often due to turnovers and inconsistent shooting.
- **Key Strengths:**
  - High-scoring offense with multiple players capable of leading in points.
  - Effective inside presence with dominant post players.
  - Strong three-point shooting capability.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Occasional turnovers leading to scoring droughts.
  - Inconsistent shooting, particularly during critical moments.
  - Challenges in maintaining leads, requiring improved game management.

## Outlook
The Michigan Wolverines have demonstrated significant improvements under Coach Dusty May, transitioning to a high-scoring, efficient offensive team. With standout performances from players like Vladislav Goldin and Danny Wolf, the team has shown promise. However, addressing issues like turnovers and shooting consistency will be crucial as they enter the NCAA tournament. Their first-round matchup is set against UC San Diego on March 20, 2025, in Denver, Colorado. A victory would set the stage for a potential clash with either Texas A&M or Yale in the subsequent round.
